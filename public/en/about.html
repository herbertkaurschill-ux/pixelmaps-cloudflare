About PixelMaps

One World · One Billion Pixels.

PixelMaps is an interactive world map made of 1 billion pixels. Every single pixel has its own fixed place on Earth – and can be secured forever by one person. Click a free point on the map, choose your coordinates and secure your pixel for €1. Every tile you buy is permanently stored on the map and is visually yours – a digital piece of the world that stays.

After your purchase you receive your pixel number and a personal certificate as a PDF by email – proof that this exact spot on the map is your pixel. Share your pixel with friends and family via a personal link.

Frequently Asked Questions

What do I get for my €1?

An exclusive pixel at coordinates you choose, permanently stored on the world map – including a personal pixel number and a certificate as a PDF by email.

How do I choose my pixel?

Click any free point on the map. The map uses real geodata (OpenStreetMap). You see your selected coordinates on screen before you start the payment.

How many pixels can I buy?

As many as you like – every single pixel costs €1. You can secure neighbouring pixels and build larger areas (e.g. your hometown or a heart on the map).

What happens after I buy?

Immediately after a successful payment your pixel is stored and shown on the map after a short processing time. You receive your pixel number and your certificate as a PDF by email.

Can I get a refund?

Yes – you have a statutory 14-day right of withdrawal. If we refund your purchase, the pixel is released again. A short email is enough (details in the terms).

How is my data handled?

We only store what is needed to run the service: your email address for the certificate and the purchase details. For details and your rights, see the Privacy Policy.

Which devices work with PixelMaps?

Desktop, tablet and smartphone – the map adapts automatically to your screen.

Why 1 billion pixels?

Our world is big – and it deserves a shared digital landmark. 1 billion pixels is not enough to map every square metre, but exactly one spot for every person on Earth… and every spot you choose.
