2. General

We offer an interactive world map on which a freely selectable geographic point (“pixel”) in a grid of 1 billion cells can be purchased against payment and displayed on the map – depending on your consent – with display name, website and message. Purchasing a pixel does not confer any rights in rem to real land; it is purely a digital collectible/decorative item.

3. What data we process

  • Display name (required): used as owner identification and – only with consent – displayed publicly on the map.
  • E-mail address (required): for sending the certificate (PDF) and purchase correspondence. The e-mail address is never shown publicly.
  • Website and message (optional): displayed together with the pixel – only with consent.
  • Coordinates / pixel number: to determine and register the purchased pixel.
  • IP address: processed briefly to prevent misuse (e.g. limiting, preventing double purchases) and stored together with the order.
  • Country/language region: via Cloudflare (cf-ipcountry / /cdn-cgi/trace) to display the local language and currency.
  • Payment data: processed exclusively by Stripe (see section 6). We do not store any card or account details.

4. Purposes and legal bases

  • Contract performance (Art. 6 (1) lit. b GDPR): registration of the pixel, creation of the certificate, sending it by e-mail, processing and proof of the purchase.
  • Consent (Art. 6 (1) lit. a GDPR): public display of display name, website and message on the map. Consent is documented with a timestamp during the ordering process. It can be revoked at any time with effect for the future (contact: section 1).
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6 (1) lit. f GDPR): prevention of misuse, spam, double purchases and fraud (including IP storage, reservation logic, amount/currency checks).
  • Legal obligations (Art. 6 (1) lit. c GDPR): retention obligations (e.g. tax retention of booking records).

5. Public display

Display name, website, message and the coordinates of the pixel are made visible to all visitors of the map – only after consent has been given. Without consent, the pixel is shown anonymously (without this information). Consent is processed separately; in that case the bare coordinate cannot be directly attributed to a person.

6. Recipients / processors / third parties

  • Cloudflare (CDN, Worker, network, location detection): privacy policy; data processing agreement; third-country transfer incl. USA (EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses).
  • Stripe (payment processing): For payment, only Stripe’s privacy policy applies. Stripe is an independent controller in this respect.
  • Resend (e-mail delivery of the certificate): processor; transfer to third countries under appropriate safeguards (SCCs).
  • OpenStreetMap / Nominatim (map tiles in the browser, server-side geocoding on click/checkout): When locating a place, the selected coordinates are sent to the server. The service provider’s usage policies apply; the data is cached briefly for the selected coordinate.
  • Content delivery (CDN) for libraries (unpkg/Leaflet, Tailwind, lucide): These scripts are loaded directly in your browser from third parties when the page loads; your IP address is transferred to these providers.

7. Storage duration

  • Order data, pixel registration and certificates: for the duration of the statutory retention obligations (in Germany e.g. 10 years for booking records), beyond that only as long as required for contract processing or accounting.
  • Proof of consent: as long as the publication exists.
  • Cancelled, never-paid orders: deleted automatically after 30 minutes.
  • IP addresses: in connection with the order; separate, time-limited storage is used where possible.

8. Local storage (localStorage)

The selected language and – for returning after payment – information about the most recently purchased pixel are stored locally in your browser. No tracking cookies are set. You can clear the local storage at any time via your browser.

9. Your rights

Under Art. 15 to 21 GDPR you have in particular the right to:

  • access to the personal data processed,
  • rectification of inaccurate data,
  • erasure (“right to be forgotten”),
  • restriction of processing,
  • data portability,
  • object to the processing,
  • withdraw granted consents with effect for the future,
  • lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (in particular the authority responsible for your place of residence or our registered office).

An e-mail to datenschutz@pixelmaps.org is sufficient to exercise these rights. Please help us to establish your identity beyond doubt (e.g. by stating your pixel number and order e-mail).

10. No automated decision-making

Decision-making based exclusively on automated processing within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR does not take place.

11. Notes for users outside the EU (incl. USA, California)

This policy primarily fulfils the requirements of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), which is applied worldwide as a minimum standard to users within the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland. If you reside outside the EU, the following applies:

  • California (CCPA/CPRA): We do not sell personal data and do not share it for behavioural advertising purposes (“Do Not Sell/Share My Personal Information”). As a California consumer you also have the rights to access, deletion and correction; contact datenschutz@pixelmaps.org for this purpose.
  • Minors: We do not target children under 13 (COPPA); purchases are restricted to persons aged 16 and over.
  • Transfer to service providers (Cloudflare, Stripe, Resend) takes place under appropriate safeguards (e.g. EU-US Data Privacy Framework, standard contractual clauses).
